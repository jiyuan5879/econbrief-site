About EconBrief

EconBrief is intended to function like a compact independent journal: public-facing, sober in tone, and structured enough to build a durable archive over time.

It is not trying to mimic institutional research. It is trying to be more readable than that, while remaining more rigorous than fast-moving online commentary.
