Observation

Profit pools do not stay fixed. They move with supply discipline, demand elasticity, regulation, substitution, and capital expenditure cycles.

What to analyze

  • Whether upstream price pressure can be passed downstream
  • Whether manufacturing margins are compressing under overcapacity

Takeaway
