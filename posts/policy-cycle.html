How Policy Shifts Reprice Risk Appetite

April 11, 2026 · EconBrief

This is a placeholder article page. Once you are ready, real reports and essays can drop into this structure without changing the design system.

Core view

  • Policy changes usually affect expectations before they affect hard data.
  • Markets front-run direction, but they cannot indefinitely front-run delivery.
  • Durability depends on whether credit and earnings eventually validate the move.

Framework

A clean way to think about the sequence is policy, liquidity, earnings, and valuation. Early relief often comes through multiple expansion; lasting moves need earnings follow-through.

Risk

If signaling and implementation diverge, or if the external liquidity backdrop tightens, repricing can stall quickly.
