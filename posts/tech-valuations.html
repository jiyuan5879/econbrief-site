How Long Can Expensive Tech Keep Working?

April 11, 2026 · EconBrief

This placeholder page shows how shorter market commentary can sit next to longer research pieces while keeping a consistent editorial look.

What matters

At high multiples, the question is rarely whether growth exists. The question is whether growth can keep surprising to the upside, and whether that surprise is worth the price already embedded in the stock.

Signals to watch

  • Revenue acceleration versus consensus expectations
  • Margin durability and capital intensity
  • Positioning, crowding, and narrative dependence

Bottom line

Expensive does not kill a stock. A broken growth narrative does.
